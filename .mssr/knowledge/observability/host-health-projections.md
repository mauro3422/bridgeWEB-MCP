# Bridge host health projections

Bridge owns the host-side daily Skill Health projection: it may persist privacy-safe structural snapshots under ignored `data/`, expose them to the local dashboard, and compute trends, but it must not store procedural skill/reference contents or autoedit skill/routing sources. From 0.6.99, the scheduler exposes the previous persisted snapshot and delegates attention transitions to packaged MSSR `evaluateMssrOperationalNoticeTransition`; Bridge only adapts candidates to `bridgeNotices`.

Bridge also owns the host-side daily Project Context Health projection over the managed workspace. Portable MSSR owns recursive repository discovery, health classification, modularization planning, and the shared Operational Notice Plane transition policy; Bridge persists metadata-only snapshots, exposes `/api/mssr/project-health`/dashboard, computes bounded structural fingerprints, and emits only meaningful REVIEW/change/resolution transitions through the existing `bridgeNotices` transport. WATCH remains quiet, stable REVIEW does not repeat just because another daily snapshot was taken, and neither scheduler nor notice may autoedit project truth.
