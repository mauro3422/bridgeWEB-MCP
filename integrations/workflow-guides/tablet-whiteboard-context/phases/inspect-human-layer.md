# inspect-human-layer

## Goal

Read the user's strokes, arrows, circles, text, grouping, spatial emphasis, and corrections as first-class evidence.

## Instructions

Before discussing embedded photos or UI, explicitly inspect hand-drawn marks and annotations: arrows, circles, underlines, crossed-out regions, labels, color distinctions, proximity, ordering, and empty space. Separate what the user drew from what ChatGPT inserted in its locked layer. Do not ignore traces merely because the embedded image is visually dominant. Mention uncertain or illegible marks instead of inventing meaning.
