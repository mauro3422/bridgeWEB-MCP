# compare-and-criticize

## Goal

Review the complete matrix, compare it with the visual references, save actionable critique per version and capture, and choose the smallest defensible correction.

## Instructions

When reference images exist, inspect those images directly before evaluating the model. Load the saved reference-observation record and compare the actual reference and actual capture together; do not infer resemblance from prompts, filenames, recipe metadata or automated scores.

Attach the master reference and accepted master captures with `image_file_attach` before human critique, using their recorded SHA-256 values. Review derivatives, thumbnails, overlays and contact sheets are diagnostics only; final judgments must inspect the original attached files. Do not substitute binary chunks, pasted Base64, local web servers, tunnels or tiny previews.

For each matched reference/render pair, record:

- visible characteristics preserved;
- visible characteristics missing;
- unintended additions;
- distorted silhouette, proportion, topology or branching;
- material, surface, VFX, growth or state differences;
- framing/projection mismatch;
- confidence and evidence capture ids.

Also save verdict, strengths, issues, next actions and capture-specific notes. Separate camera, transport, subject-presence, geometry, silhouette, proportion, material, animation, state, readability, integration, performance and cleanup failures.

Do not approve from one attractive close-up. Do not mark a reference-driven asset complete without valid subject-presence checks, readable reference analysis and written paired-image comparison. The critique must state what to preserve from the baseline and exactly what to change next.
