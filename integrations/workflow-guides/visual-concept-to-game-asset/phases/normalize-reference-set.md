# normalize-reference-set

## Goal

Convert the selected direction into preserved, inspectable references for modeling and later comparison.

## Instructions

Preserve the original images and exact generation prompts without overwriting them. Choose a master view and obtain front, profile, top, three-quarter, scale, growth or state references when applicable.

For every master/reference image, inspect the image itself and save a structured observation record containing:

- visible silhouette and composition;
- relative width, height, depth cues and dominant proportions;
- primary and secondary masses;
- topology, branching, repetition and attachment relationships;
- material, color/value, roughness and surface cues;
- VFX, spores, glow, motion or growth cues;
- scale/context and intended readability distance;
- characteristics that must survive production;
- characteristics that may be simplified;
- visible contradictions between views;
- uncertainties and hypotheses about hidden geometry.

Do not copy claims from the prompt into the observation record unless the generated image actually shows them. Mark generated-view inconsistencies and decide which view controls each disputed characteristic.
