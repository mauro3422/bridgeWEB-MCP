# Character design-master prompt

Use this after locking `character-brief.json`.

```text
Create the canonical full-body FRONT-RIGHT THREE-QUARTER DESIGN MASTER of the original character described below.

IDENTITY LOCK
{{identityLock}}

DESIGN AUTHORITY
- preserve the exact species, face, body proportions, palette, markings, clothing, accessories, hands, feet, tail count and tail base described in the brief
- clearly communicate material transitions, torso depth, limb volumes, facial structure and how accessories attach
- use low-poly-friendly, readable forms suitable for later 3D reconstruction

POSE AND CAMERA
- one complete character only
- relaxed neutral standing pose suitable for comparison with orthographic construction views
- controlled front-right three-quarter angle, level camera and moderate lens language
- full body visible from highest feature to feet
- arms separated from torso and hands readable
- legs separated enough to read the silhouette

PRESENTATION
- clean neutral or pure-white background
- soft neutral studio lighting
- no scenery, text, labels, border, watermark or unrelated prop
- no cast shadow hiding the feet or baseline

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS
- no redesign, alternate outfit, changed markings or changed colors
- no extra limbs, digits, ears, horns or tails
- no cropped extremities
- no action pose, dutch angle, extreme foreshortening or dramatic perspective
```

This image owns visual identity, materials and volume intent. It is not the geometric measurement authority.
