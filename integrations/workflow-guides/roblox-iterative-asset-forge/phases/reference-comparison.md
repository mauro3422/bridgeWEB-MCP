# reference-comparison

## Goal

Persist references, renders, overlays, contours, metrics, and a human-readable critique.

## Instructions

Copy the original concept images into the repository with hashes. Treat silhouette metrics as diagnostics, not beauty scores. Review front, side, top, three-quarter, growth/state strip, and animation extremes where relevant.

Attach the master concept and accepted master renders with `image_file_attach`, including their recorded SHA-256 values, before writing the visual comparison. Do not use binary chunks, copied Base64, temporary servers, tunnels or tiny previews as substitutes for the original evidence.
