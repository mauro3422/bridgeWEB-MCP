## Changes

- Adopt exact `@mauroprime/mssr 0.2.70` from `vendor/mauroprime-mssr-0.2.70.tgz`; the vendored artifact SHA-256 is `73b25634781b4884a84fe2cbfbaa2f3e0b1c66f92dde0d687eee8ac1886d9f35`.
- Extend `skill_bootstrap` with bounded `retainedContextObligations` receipts (`id` + exact content `fingerprint`). Receipts are caller attestations for procedural units still present in the current uncompacted context; Bridge never reconstructs them from historical trace loads, checkpoints, delivery receipts, or project state.
- Forward the exact receipt set to portable MSSR 0.2.70 and preserve it only inside the in-memory continuation lease for the same opaque cursor chain. Restart, handoff, compaction, expiry, or receipt omission therefore fails open to normal re-delivery.
- Surface retained-context accounting in the compact response (`retainedContextCharsSaved`, accepted retained units, and per-skill `contextSatisfied`/retained units) while keeping selected-context accounting exact.
- Preserve the public `loaded[]` meaning across paging: units delivered on earlier pages are not repeated merely because portable MSSR reports the skill lifecycle-satisfied; first-page skills satisfied explicitly by current retention receipts remain visible with empty re-delivered content.
- Keep R1 immutable trace-owner isolation and R2 automatic lifecycle preflight unchanged.
