## Changes

- `mssr-trace-context` now consumes successful `skill_context_next.loaded[]` results for the exact returned trace and applies them through the portable `reduceMssrSkillLoadLifecycle(...)` reducer.
- A continuation for another trace cannot satisfy obligations on the active trace; trace owner identity and R1 fail-closed semantics are unchanged.
- Delegated `bridge_tool_query` continuation keeps forwarding the explicit wrapper/control `traceId` into the trace-aware `skill_context_next` target.
- Added a focused trace-contract regression for a two-page required-skill route: page one leaves one required skill pending, a foreign continuation changes nothing, and the matching continuation clears the remaining obligation in RAM.
- Updated the indexed trace lifecycle knowledge module and Bridge incident log with the demonstrated RAM-vs-durable lifecycle mismatch and its correction.
