## Changes

- `skill_route_plan` and `skill_bootstrap` now accept an optional bounded `contextMessages` batch plus normal message/count character budgets.
- Bridge validates messages through the strict portable MSSR schema and delegates stage/intent selection, required-message overflow, dedupe, and continuation receipt semantics to MSSR.
- Responses preserve the complete portable selection and decision evidence. Only selected messages are converted once into the existing internal Bridge notice envelope for normal per-response piggyback delivery.
- Canonical owner, provenance, freshness, evidence references, continuation receipts, review-required persistence proposals, and advisory actions remain observable data. Bridge does not execute advisory actions, grant permissions, or persist proposals.
- Portable `attention` maps to Bridge `info`; severity is never elevated by the adapter.
- Added focused plan/bootstrap regression coverage for selection, dedupe, strict rejection, evidence preservation, severity mapping, and the non-execution boundary.
