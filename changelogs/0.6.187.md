## Changes

- Add `bridge_cross_repo_transaction` with exact transaction confirmation, owner/trace/root/ref checks, bounded source reads, a durable integrity-checked journal, staged SHA-256 readback, no-overwrite destination creation, replay-safe recovery, conservative rollback, and a consumer-pointer receipt before commit.
- Block declared cross-repository writes through the generic `bridge_tool_action`; use the dedicated journaled transaction path. MSSR ownership decisions remain advisory and do not grant host authorization.
- Preserve lifecycle preflight denials in Notice Plane history and deliver them to the next compatible bootstrap. When `traceId` is absent, correlate only by observed owner and workflow; ambiguous routes stay blocked and do not count as executed writes.
- Reproduce the historic no-`traceId` pattern in an isolated project: two compatible routes remain separate; after both age beyond the 90-second dominance window, a new owner/workflow continuation abstains, returns `executed=false`, and records an owner/workflow-scoped notice.
