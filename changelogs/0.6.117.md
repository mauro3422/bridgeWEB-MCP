- Compact remaining-unit and global-module metadata avoids repeating selection detail already represented by the cursor and loaded page.

Explicit notice-drain tools retain their requested limit.

- Does not read or change SSH configuration, keys, credentials, `.env`, authentication stores or remote-node identity.
