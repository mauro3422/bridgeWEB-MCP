# review-relations-and-conflicts

## Goal

Surface support, duplication, contradiction and unresolved relations without silently merging claims.

## Instructions

Create or use only explicitly source-bound EvidenceAtoms and exact sourceEvidence. Supply claim comparability with scope and time/validity fields. Use mssr_semantic_evidence_relation_review for bounded typed relation decisions. Jev judgments remain unverified: independently inspect the cited ranges and preserve scope, time, negation and uncertainty. Missing comparability, conflicting evidence or unresolved decisions remain in review; do not drop the losing side or authorize a merge.
